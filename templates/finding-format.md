## Output format (mandatory)
For each hypothesis:
- ID: <CLASS>-<n> (e.g., AC-1)
- Location: file.sol:line
- Hypothesis: one sentence (who attacks, what they call, what they gain)
- Preconditions:
- Estimated impact: High / Medium / Low, justified in terms of value at risk
- Confidence: High / Medium / Low
- How to prove: what a Foundry PoC would need to demonstrate
If nothing is found, write "No hypotheses" and list what you checked.
